MIGRATION RECOMMENDATIONS
- Protect the homepage's charter relevance while making sales the first commercial journey.
- Include actual yacht detail pages in the 70–75-page inventory rather than hiding all yacht detail in expandable cards.
- Review availability and accuracy of every yacht listing before deciding retention. Do not equate absent keyword data with no value.
- Retain or carefully map /buy-a-yacht/, /buy-a-yacht/sell-your-yacht/, /yacht-management/, charter hub, event pages and useful articles.
- Review /how-much-does-a-yacht-cost/ and /new-years-eve-in-abu-dhabi/ for factual updates and retain their useful content and established URLs where practical.
- Decide the treatment of existing Dubai and Qatar pages explicitly; omission from the new priority-market list does not itself justify deletion.
- Keep /whatsapp working as a utility redirect; exclude it, RSS feeds and tracking parameters from the content-page target.
- Build the final old-to-new URL map only after inventory availability, service coverage and content decisions are confirmed. Do not redirect unrelated retired content to the homepage.
- Same-URL language switching serves visitors but should not be represented as equivalent to separately indexable language URLs.
